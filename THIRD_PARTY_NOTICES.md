## Translated sources

The FairPlay framing constants, PlayFair table assets and the FairPlay transforms are
translations of GPL/LGPL-licensed C sources, including `omg_hax.c`, `modified_md5.c`,
`playfair.c`, `sap_hash.c` and `hand_garble.c`. The original `fairplay_playfair.c` carries the
LGPL-2.1-or-later notice of Juho Vähä-Herttua; the `playfair/` files carry no per-file header, so
their Rust translations (`src/playfair.rs`, `src/playfair_tables.rs`,
`src/playfair/hand_garble_generated.rs`) use the same LGPL-2.1-or-later notice. Each translated
file keeps the applicable SPDX identifier.

The receiver's wire behaviour - discovery records, RTSP routes, RTP framing, the mirror
keystream, pairing and the timing exchange - was established from the behaviour of an existing
GPL AirPlay receiver. Those implementations are not vendored here and are not linked into this
crate; what is derived from them is the protocol contract, recorded in the tests.

Retain the original copyright and licence notices when translating or incorporating another
source. Record the source files and notices here before such a change.

## Test data

The property lists in `tests/fixtures/` are generated by the CPython standard library
`plistlib` module through `tools/plist_fixtures.py`. They are synthetic test data, and no CPython
or plistlib code is linked into the product. Test vectors constructed in this project are
synthetic and are not device captures.

## Dependencies

- The plist layer depends on [`plist`](https://crates.io/crates/plist) 1.8.0 (MIT), which reads
  and writes the binary and XML property-list formats. `Cargo.lock` also pins its transitive
  dependencies (`quick-xml`, `indexmap`, `hashbrown`, `time`, `base64ct`, ...). Version,
  licence and MSRV details are in `Cargo.lock` and `docs/BUILD.md`.
- The network control service depends on [`tokio`](https://crates.io/crates/tokio) 1.53.1 (MIT)
  with the `rt-multi-thread`, `net`, `time`, `sync`, `io-util` and `macros` features, together
  with `bytes`, `mio`, `pin-project-lite`, `socket2`, `tokio-macros` and `windows-sys`. The
  choice, its alternatives and the MSRV evidence are in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- The pairing service draws its random values - the one-time PIN, the SRP salt and private
  value, and the X25519 key pair of `/pair-verify` - from
  [`getrandom`](https://crates.io/crates/getrandom) 0.2.15 (MIT OR Apache-2.0). It was already
  pinned as a transitive dependency of `rand_core` before it was declared directly, so the
  dependency set of the product is unchanged.
- Discovery depends on [`mdns-sd`](https://crates.io/crates/mdns-sd) 0.21.5 (Apache-2.0 OR MIT),
  a multicast-DNS responder written in Rust. `Cargo.lock` also pins its transitive dependencies
  (`if-addrs`, `socket-pktinfo`, `socket2`, `flume`, `log`, `parking_lot`/`lock_api`,
  `scopeguard`, `spin`, ...). The record contents this module advertises follow the AirPlay
  discovery contract, which is what makes a stock client accept the receiver.
- The renderer boundary has no dependencies: it is the trait a backend implements, the frame and
  configuration types a session hands across it, and a renderer that accepts everything and
  plays nothing (`NullRenderer`).
